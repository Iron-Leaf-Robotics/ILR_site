About this report

Marked by operator
Stretches of row (the bands on the map) where the operator marked gopher activity while pushing the cart.
Photos
Camera frames checked by a reviewer; the box shows the mound. 13 of the 14 marks have one.
Locations
The row the cart was in, and the distance from the row end named in the checklist. Approximate: the cart uses standard GPS, not survey-grade.
Confirmed, and what's left to check
A reviewer confirmed each mound shown in the camera frames. Marks without a frame, and whether a burrow is still active, are for the crew to check on the ground.
Grower data
Growers own the raw imagery from their farm. We keep a copy to train our detection models, unless the grower asks us to delete it after training.
